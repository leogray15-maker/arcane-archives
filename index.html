FAQ

Straight answers.

Is this just more videos?

No. It’s a private membership: a community, a live call every week, the Trading Floor and Watchtower, and a vault of 3,330+ modules across four realms. The videos are the smallest part of it.

Why is there an assessment?

Because the room only works if everyone in it is serious. Twelve questions, about three minutes. Clear it and you get instant access.

What exactly do I get?

Everything on the membership card above: the whole vault, the private community, daily quests, weekly War Room calls, the platform tools, and every update while you’re a member.

How does billing work?

£128 a month. Cancel anytime, no contract. There are no refunds once you’ve been given access, which keeps the room full of people who actually meant it.

I’m a complete beginner. Will I be lost?

No. Everyone starts as a Seeker with the same first mission. Each domain runs in order from the ground up, and the weekly call is there for anything that doesn’t click.

How is this different from free content?

Free content is built to keep you watching, so it gives you the what, one tactic at a time. The Archives gives you the why, in order, with a room of people doing it alongside you.

Is the trading content financial advice?

No. Everything in the Archives is education. Nothing inside is a recommendation to buy or sell anything, and trading involves risk of loss.

What if I have more questions?

Message us on Telegram or email. A real person replies. But if you’ve read this far, you probably already know.
